# Product Definition

## Purpose
- Align product, design, content, and engineering decisions around a single source of truth for the Chen landing website.

## Product Vision
- Build a high-converting, trustworthy digital front door for the clinic that helps visitors understand treatments clearly and book consultations with confidence.

## Target Users
- Primary users:
  - Adults (roughly ages 25-55) interested in aesthetic and skin-quality treatments.
  - Users comparing treatment options and looking for safe, professional guidance.
  - Mobile-first visitors who want quick answers and an easy booking path.
- Secondary users:
  - Returning patients checking follow-up or additional treatment options.
  - Clinic staff who need consistent lead quality and clearer user intent from inquiries.

## Problem Statement
- Potential clients often feel overwhelmed by treatment choices, unclear differences between services, and uncertainty about suitability. Existing clinic websites frequently provide fragmented information, weak trust signals, and slow booking flows, causing drop-off before users complete a consultation request.

## Value Proposition
- The site provides clear, structured treatment information, strong trust cues, and a fast path to consultation.
- Visitors can quickly understand categories, compare services, and take action with minimal friction.
- The experience is optimized for both mobile and desktop, with content architecture designed for confidence and conversion.

## Product Scope
- In scope:
  - Landing and section-based homepage experience.
  - Treatment category and service pages.
  - Contact and consultation conversion flows.
  - FAQ and informational support content.
  - Basic analytics tracking for page views and conversion events.
- Out of scope:
  - Full patient portal or account system.
  - Online payments or complex checkout.
  - Real-time live chat operations.
  - Personalized treatment recommendation engine (phase 1).

## Success Metrics
- Business metrics:
  - Consultation lead volume per week.
  - Qualified lead rate (leads matching target treatment categories).
  - Consultation-to-booked-treatment conversion rate.
- Product metrics:
  - CTA click-through rate (homepage and treatment pages).
  - Contact form completion rate.
  - Bounce rate reduction on key landing and treatment pages.
  - Mobile engagement quality (time on page, scroll depth, completion rate).
- Baseline and targets:
  - Baseline: collect 2-4 weeks of analytics after instrumentation validation.
  - Initial targets (first optimization cycle):
    - +20% CTA click-through rate on core pages.
    - +15% contact/consultation form completion rate.
    - -10% bounce rate on top entry pages.

## Constraints and Assumptions
- Constraints:
  - Nuxt 3 static-first deployment flow.
  - Limited content and design bandwidth per iteration.
  - Compliance-safe language and conservative medical/clinical claims.
- Assumptions:
  - Most traffic is mobile and arrives from social/search campaigns.
  - Users prefer short, clear explanations before submitting a consultation request.
  - Trust elements (credentials, clarity, consistency) have direct impact on conversion.

## Prioritization Rules
- Prioritize changes that increase consultation intent and completion.
- Prefer improvements that reduce user confusion on treatment selection.
- Prioritize high-traffic, high-drop-off pages before low-impact refinements.
- Favor low-risk, measurable iterations over broad redesigns.
- Defer features without clear conversion or trust impact.
